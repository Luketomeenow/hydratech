Sales of bottom hole sampling cylinders & equipment’s

Bottom hole sampling involves capturing reservoir fluid samples at reservoir conditions. To obtain accurate compositional and PVT analyses of these samples, the recovered samples must remain in bottom hole conditions. Special chambers compensate for the pressure drop as the samples are returned to the surface. Our sister concern Habu sampling, Norway manufacturing both oil and gas bottom hole sampling cylinders. we are supplying the same to Gulf region.

Gas cylinders have a capacity of 20 liters at 3000 PSI. Oil cylinders have a capacity of 1000 CC at 10000 PSI.
