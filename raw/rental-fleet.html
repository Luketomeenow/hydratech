Rental fleet

Flushing equipment’s & particle counter

We have a Rental fleet for Flushing Equipment’s and particle counter which measure the purity/NAS class of oil. All our flushing rigs are fabricated with High quality motors/Engine and pumps. We have below equipment at our rental fleet

  • Oil flushing equipment
  • water flushing equipment
  • laser particle counter for online analysis

High pressure testing equipment’s & Chart recorders

We are supplying both High pressure and low pressure testing pumps on rental basis. We have a pressure testing skids up to 35000 PSI. Pressure gauge and pressure chart recorders are mostly inbuilt with skids.

Filter skids

Filter skids which we supply can be used for water, chemical, fuel filtrations. All filter housings are fabricated with stainless steel and connected with carbon steel pipes. Each filter housing (bag) have a capacity up to 20m3/hour. We have 4bag, 8 bag and 12 bag skids & multi bag filter skids which have 8 bags in common housing.

Oil filtration pumps

Oil filtration is the industry norm for removing contamination from oil to achieve maximum cleanliness class. Hydratech’ s offline filtration unit can filter Hydraulic/Lube system up to 2 micron level.

Gas booster pumps are designed for wide range of applications. Oxygen, Nitrogen boosting, gas filling, pressurization & oil free gas compression can be done by Gas boosters.

Gas boosters

Gas booster pumps are designed for wide range of applications. Oxygen, Nitrogen boosting, gas filling, pressurization & oil free gas compression can be done by Gas boosters.

Oil & gas sampling cylinders

We are supplying bottom Hole sampling cylinders on rental basis for both oil & gas sampling which is designed to store oil/gas sample and transport to laboratory for testing purpose.

Gas cylinders have a capacity of 20 liters at 3000 PSI. Oil cylinders have a capacity of 1000 CC at 10000 PSI.
